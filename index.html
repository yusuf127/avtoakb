1. High-voltage battery at the rear

What the driver notices:

The charge on the display quickly reaches maximum and drops to two bars within minutes, a hybrid system warning lights up on the dashboard, and fuel consumption rises.

How we check it in the bay:

We connect a specialist scanner, check the voltage spread across cell pairs under load, and inspect the copper contacts and the cleanliness of the cooling fan.

When to come in for a check

Four typical situations for hybrid owners in Bishkek when you should not put off a diagnostic.

Situation 01

A hybrid system warning lit up on the dashboard

A red triangle or a hybrid system fault message appeared. Do not disconnect the battery terminal to reset it — during diagnostics it is important to see the stored code and the conditions under which the fault occurred.

Situation 02

The battery charges fast and immediately drops to two bars

A direct sign of capacity loss in individual cells of the high-voltage battery. At an early stage the problem can often be solved by replacing weak cells and cleaning the busbars, without replacing the whole pack.

Situation 03

Fuel consumption is up and the car rarely switches to electric drive

The combustion engine almost never shuts off at traffic lights and in traffic, and the electric motor barely helps when accelerating. We check battery temperature, the cooling fan and engine fuel trims.

Situation 04

Inspecting a hybrid before buying at a market or privately

Before a sale, dashboard errors are often cleared with a basic scanner and come back after 30–50 km. We check the battery’s real remaining life under load and the inverter’s condition before any money changes hands.
